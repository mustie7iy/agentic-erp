# ADR-0004: UUIDv7 identifiers plus document numbers from number ranges

**Status:** proposed

## Context
Ids must be unique across cells and services, generated without a central
sequence, and index-friendly. Business users need readable, sometimes
legally gapless, document numbers.

## Decision
- Every entity has `id uuid` generated by the application as **UUIDv7**
  (time-ordered, so B-tree inserts stay local).
- Business documents also carry a `document_number` from a **number range**
  owned by the configuration context: per tenant, legal entity, document
  type and fiscal year, with a `gapless` flag. Gapless ranges allocate inside
  the posting transaction (row lock on the range); gapped ranges allocate in
  blocks cached by the service.
- Migrated SAP records keep their SAP keys in `external_refs`
  (system, object, key) for traceability.

## Alternatives
- **bigint sequences:** compact but cell- and service-local; collisions on merge.
- **UUIDv4:** random inserts fragment indexes at scale.

## Consequences
- 16-byte keys; acceptable.
- Gapless numbering serializes postings per range; ranges are per legal
  entity and type, so contention is bounded.
